## Target: client-onboarding Bundle 5 (lifecycle events)

- **B4-D-opsidempotency1** [STATUS: OPEN] `alertFounder`/`createCheckin` (`src/lib/onboarding/ops.ts`)
  have no crash-window idempotency/reconcile protection — unlike the board (marker-based reconcile) and
  email (idempotency key), which the spec explicitly requires it for. The gap: if the Redis write in
  `persistStep` fails for a reason OTHER than lease loss (e.g. a transient Upstash blip) right after
  `alertFounder`/`createCheckin` succeeds, a retry will re-send the alert / re-create the check-in card.
  Reason this target is right: Bundle 5 already touches the ops board + alerts (lifecycle events —
  subscription cancellation/pause need their own founder-facing alert), making it the natural place to
  design the reconcile/dedup vocabulary once, for both bundles' alert/card paths, rather than bolting on
  a possibly-wrong version under this bundle's time pressure. A naive fix (e.g. scanning the ops board's
  "To Do" list by title) would be actively WRONG on Bruno's manually-curated ops board — he moves/archives
  cards himself, so a completed-and-archived check-in card could get silently duplicated by a title scan.
  Needs a real design decision (what "reconciled" means for an alert email and for a card that may have
  moved lists), not a mechanical fix.
