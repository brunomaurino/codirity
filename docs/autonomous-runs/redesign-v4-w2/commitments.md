## Target: redesign-v4 Bundle W3

(none as deferrals — awareness:)

- **The band is a bare `<section>`, not `<Section>`.** It sets `data-ground="dark"` itself so the
  folio's `on-light` detection still works. W1's commitment said the opposite ("must keep rendering
  through `Section`") — nesting `.wrap-v4` inside `Section`'s own `px-4 md:px-8` is precisely what
  doubled the page gutter and caused this bundle's BLOCKER. **Any W3+ section that owns a full-bleed
  ground should follow W1's hero and W2's band: bare `<section>` + `data-ground` + `.wrap-v4`, and
  never a `.wrap-v4` inside a padded container.**
- `Section`'s `ink` variant is now v4-correct (`bg-ground text-chalk`) and the v3 one-per-site rule
  is formally superseded — W3–W6 may use dark sections freely.
- **A "no horizontal overflow" check is not a layout gate.** `body { overflow-x: hidden }` makes it
  structurally incapable of failing on intra-grid collision — it produced a clean pass over the
  exact band where the BLOCKER lived. Measure **element bounding boxes**, and force a collision to
  prove the gate fires before trusting a green result.
- The boundary lesson (now four strikes) still binds: measure at the exact breakpoint in-browser.
  W2's `860px` came from the mockup verbatim rather than arithmetic, and was re-measured at
  859/860/861.
