## Target: redesign-v4 Bundle W6 (ownership, close, footer, retirement sweep)

(none as deferrals — awareness:)

- **Every dark-ground text token needs its paper twin wired to the GROUND, not remembered at the
  call site.** W5 shipped `.lede` (a `--chalk-dim` token) onto paper at 1.76:1 as a BLOCKER, while
  a `.lede-ink` utility sat unused in the repo for exactly that case. `.paper .label` and
  `.paper .lede` now bind by ground; **W6 must do the same for any token it renders on paper**, and
  the sweep should check whether any OTHER dark-ground utility is reachable from a light section.
- **`Section`'s `default` variant now paints `--paper`, not `bg-white`.** The `.band-*` gradients
  terminate at `--paper`, so a white section adjacent to a band leaves a seam. Its only consumer is
  the v3 Benefits survivor — if W6 retires or reworks Benefits, keep the ground consistent.
- **`.band-ld`/`.band-dl` now appear FOUR times** and the page is two runs (dark: clients →
  studies → terms → queue; paper: what we build → how it works → founder → FAQ). W6's ownership
  block and close sit on dark after the paper run, so the paper→dark band before them already
  exists — do not add a second.
- **The declined-row strike is an INLINE box wrapping the text with
  `box-decoration-break: clone`,** not the mockup's absolutely-positioned `scaleX` bar. The mockup's
  version silently assumed a single-line row and struck only line 1 on the three rows that wrap at
  375px. Any future "draw a line over text" effect has the same trap.
- **New tokens must be aliased into `@theme inline`** or a `text-<token>` utility is a silent no-op.
  `--chalk-faint` (W3) and `--ink-faint` (W5) both shipped un-aliased.
- **Now orphaned and safe to delete in the sweep:** `AccentWord` and `SectionHeader` lose their last
  consumers when Benefits and ContactInfo are reworked (they are still live today). `ProcessStep`
  and `Badge` were already deleted here.
