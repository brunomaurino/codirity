## FAQ answers directories often ask for

- **How fast?** Most individual tasks ship within a few days; larger builds are broken into milestones.
- **Who writes the code?** Engineers. The AI drafts; a senior engineer reads, changes and signs off on everything that ships.
- **Who owns the code?** The client: repos in their org, infrastructure in their cloud accounts.
- **Contracts?** None. Pause or cancel anytime.
- **Response time?** We reply within 24 hours.
